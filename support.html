What the app does

Tier One Outreach gives you the Compliance approved emails, texts and social posts for Tier One Direct, with your unique Fast Track link already in every piece. You load the people you already know, pick one, and send from your own email or messaging app. You never write or edit the message copy, which is what keeps it approved.

Signing in

Enter the email address Aflac has on file for you and tap Send code. A six digit code arrives by email. Enter it and you are in. There is no password.

  • No code arrived. Check your spam folder. If it is still missing after a few minutes, tap Send code again. Codes expire quickly, so use the newest one.
  • It says your email is not on the pilot list. Ask your MKD or MKT to have you added, then try again.
  • The code will not take. Codes are single use and short lived. Request a fresh one rather than reusing an old email.

Your unique link

Paste the link from your Fast Track onboarding email into the Your link box, or tap Scan QR code and point the camera at the QR code in that email. Every email, text and post then carries it. If the link box is empty, your pieces fall back to a generic address and you get no credit, so set it before you send anything.

Loading contacts

  • In the app. Tap Add from contacts, allow the permission, then search and check the people you want. Only the people you check are saved.
  • From a file. Upload a CSV with columns First, Last, Email, Phone, or a vCard file. On an iPhone you can open Contacts, select people, tap Share, send the card to yourself, and open it in the app.
  • Declined the permission. You can turn it back on in your phone settings under the app, or use the file import instead.

Sending

Double click anyone in your list to open the approved pieces for them. Pick Email, Text or Social, then tap Gmail, Outlook, Send as text, or a copy button. Your own mail or messaging app opens with the message filled in, and you press send.

  • Gmail opens empty. Make sure the Gmail app is installed and you are signed in to it. If it still opens blank, use Copy formatted email and paste into a new message.
  • A contact shows no email pieces. That person either has no email address on file or has unsubscribed. Text and social pieces still work.

Your account

The Account tab shows the email you signed in with, lets you correct the name that appears in your messages, and has the Sign out button. Your contacts and link follow your account, so signing in on a second device shows the same list.

What we cannot help with

Questions about commissions, appointments, product availability or a specific customer's policy go to your DSC or MKD, not to this address. We support the app itself.
