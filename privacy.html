Tier One Outreach

Privacy Policy

Operated by OTB Solutions, LLC. Last updated September 17, 2026.

The short version. Tier One Outreach helps an Aflac associate send Compliance approved messages to people they already know. The app reads the contacts you choose so it can address a message to that person. Your contacts stay in your own account, they are never sold, and no other associate can see them.

Who operates this app

Tier One Outreach is operated by OTB Solutions, LLC, a Georgia limited liability company. You can reach us at chris@solutionotb.com.

The app is used by independent agents representing Aflac as part of a pilot program. The insurance products described in the messages are offered by Tier One Insurance Company. This policy covers the app itself, not those companies' own websites or products.

Who can use it

The app is for Aflac associates in the pilot. Only email addresses on the pilot list can create an account. It is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

What the app collects

  • Your account. The email address you sign in with, your name as it appears in the messages you send, your unique Fast Track link, and the business mailing address required in the email footer.
  • Your contacts. First and last name, email address and phone number for the people you add. You add them by selecting them from your phone's address book, importing a CSV or vCard file, or typing them in.
  • Your activity. A record of which approved piece you sent, through which channel, and when. We keep this so the pilot can be measured.
  • Unsubscribe requests. If a recipient clicks Unsubscribe in one of your emails, we record that address so the app stops offering email pieces for that person.

The app does not collect location, photos, browsing history, or advertising identifiers. It contains no advertising and no third party trackers.

How contacts are handled

When you tap Add from contacts, your phone asks your permission first. If you allow it, the app reads names, email addresses and phone numbers so it can show you a list to choose from. Only the people you select are saved. The rest are never sent anywhere and are not retained.

Contacts are used for one purpose: to fill in a recipient's first name and to address an email or text to that person. Contacts are never sold, rented, shared with Aflac or Tier One Insurance Company, used to build a marketing list, or used to train any model.

The app never writes to, changes or deletes anything in your phone's address book. On Android the contacts permission is declared as a read and write pair because the plugin the app uses groups them together; the app only reads.

How messages are actually sent

The app does not send email or text messages itself. When you tap Gmail, Outlook or Send as text, the app opens that app on your phone with the approved message already filled in, and you press send. The message goes from your own account, and we do not receive a copy of it.

Where the information is stored

Account details, contacts and the activity record are stored in a Postgres database hosted by Supabase in the United States. Access is restricted per account, so you can only read and write your own rows. A copy is also cached on your device so the app works when you have no signal.

The pilot administrator at OTB Solutions can see the list of associate accounts and the activity record. The administrator cannot read your contacts.

How long it is kept

Your information is kept while you have an account. When the pilot ends, or when you ask us to remove your account, we delete your account, your contacts and your cached data. Write to chris@solutionotb.com and we will confirm once it is done.

You can also remove any individual contact inside the app at any time, which deletes that record.

Your choices

  • You can decline the contacts permission and still use the app by importing a file or typing contacts in.
  • You can sign out from the Account tab, which clears the copy stored on that device.
  • You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to the address above.

Recipients of your messages

Every approved email carries an Unsubscribe link. Clicking it records the opt out and shows a short confirmation page. A person who has unsubscribed no longer appears as an email option in your app. If someone wants their information removed entirely, write to us and we will remove it.

Security

Sign in uses a one time code sent to your email address. There is no password to steal. All traffic uses HTTPS, and database access is restricted per account. No system is perfect, so tell us right away if you think an account has been misused.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will also be sent to pilot participants by email.
